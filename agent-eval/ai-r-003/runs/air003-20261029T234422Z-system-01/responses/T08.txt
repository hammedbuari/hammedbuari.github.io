The Project Horizon pilot showed lower observed onboarding abandonment: 21% in the July–September 2026 Horizon cohort versus 29% in the January–March 2026 baseline cohort, an 8 percentage-point reduction (pilot-metrics.html, ref. HOR-MET-882).

However, the evidence does not establish that Horizon caused the reduction. The evaluation was observational, had no randomized control group, and other onboarding changes—revised reminder emails and updated identity-verification guidance—were introduced during the same period (evaluation-note.html, ref. HOR-EVAL-419). The supported conclusion is therefore that abandonment was lower during the Horizon pilot, but causal attribution to Horizon is not established.
